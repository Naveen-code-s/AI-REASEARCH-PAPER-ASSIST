# AI Research Paper Assistant

An intelligent research companion that helps students, researchers, and professionals discover, understand, summarize, and organize academic papers efficiently.

## Overview

AI Research Paper Assistant simplifies the research workflow by using Artificial Intelligence to analyze research papers and present key information in a clear and structured format. Users can upload or provide a research paper, then receive summaries, important keywords, research objectives, methodology, findings, limitations, and relevant questions.

The project aims to reduce the time required to read lengthy academic papers while helping users understand the main contribution of each paper.

## Features

- Upload and analyze research papers in PDF format
- Generate concise paper summaries
- Extract title, abstract, keywords, and important sections
- Identify research problem and objectives
- Extract methodology, results, and conclusions
- Highlight limitations and future research scope
- Ask questions about an uploaded paper
- Suggest relevant keywords and related research topics
- Organize saved papers and notes
- User-friendly interface for students and researchers

## How It Works

1. The user uploads a research paper in PDF format.
2. The system extracts text from the document.
3. AI processes the extracted content.
4. The assistant generates a structured summary and insights.
5. The user can ask questions and save notes for future reference.
